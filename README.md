# 🌿 CHAMELEON PROTOCOL: THE LIVING LABYRINTH

Welcome to **Chameleon Protocol: The Living Labyrinth**—a reimagined evolution of the interactive world concept, where every wall breathes, every corridor shifts, and your choices permanently reshape the environment around you. Unlike conventional static game worlds, this repository houses a **dynamic architectural simulation engine** that treats the game map as a sentient organism.

This is not a sequel. This is a metamorphosis—a complete reimagining of how players interact with procedurally generated spaces. Instead of a fixed environment, you navigate a living, breathing maze that learns from your movement patterns, adapts to your playstyle, and actively evolves its own geometry to challenge or assist you.

---

## 🦎 What Makes This Different?

Most games ask you to conquer a world. **Chameleon Protocol** asks you to *become* the world. Your every action triggers a chain of environmental responses:

- **Memory-Based Terrain**: Walls remember where you've walked and subtly shift to create new pathways
- **Emotional Resonance Zones**: Areas change color and texture based on player proximity and recent actions
- **Adaptive Difficulty Corridors**: Paths widen when you're struggling, narrow when you're breezing through
- **Symbiotic NPC Habitats**: Creatures that inhabit the maze form ecosystems that evolve with your behavior

The core innovation here is the **Morphological Feedback Loop**—a system where player input doesn't just affect the game state, but physically rewrites the underlying architecture of the playable space.

## 🎮 Core Gameplay Experience

### The Living Map System
Forget static minimaps. Your HUD displays a **pulsing topographical overlay** that changes in real-time as the environment breathes. Corridors swell and contract, dead ends bloom into new chambers, and hidden alcoves reveal themselves only when you achieve specific gameplay milestones.

### Adaptive Challenge Mechanics
The labyrinth doesn't just sit there—it **tests you**:
- When you solve puzzles quickly, walls retract to create faster routes
- When you struggle with combat, environmental hazards temporarily reduce their intensity
- When you explore thoroughly, secret areas merge into main pathways

### Color-Language Communication
The entire visual language of the game speaks through hues and patterns:
- **Sapphire pulses** indicate nearby points of interest you haven't discovered
- **Amber ripples** warn of approaching environmental shifts
- **Violet veins** show the "memories" of your past journeys
